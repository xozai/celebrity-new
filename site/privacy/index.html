Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Celebrity Celebrity is a free, privacy-focused iOS party game developed by xozai. We believe party games should be fun, simple, and respect your privacy. The app requires no user account, collects no personal information, and includes no advertising or tracking SDKs.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect or store:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or physical location.
  • Device identifiers used for advertising or cross-app tracking (IDFA).
  • Contacts, camera photos, or personal files.
  • Payment or financial details (the app is completely free).

2. How the Game Operates (Anonymous Authentication)

To enable real-time multiplayer games across devices, the app connects to Supabase using anonymous authentication.

  • Anonymous Identifier: On launch, an anonymous cryptographic user ID is created solely to distinguish players within a room, assign teams, and rotate clue-givers. This identifier is never linked to any personal identity.
  • Room Codes & Player Display Names: When you enter a room, the nickname you choose and the 4-character room code are stored temporarily in the database to coordinate the game for all players in that room.
  • Game Slips: The celebrity names and slips submitted by players are stored temporarily in the database for the duration of the game.

3. Data Retention and Automatic Deletion

All game session data is strictly temporary:

  • Finished games are automatically and permanently deleted within 1 hour of game completion.
  • Any room idle for 24 hours is automatically purged.
  • Delete My Data: Players can delete their active session and data at any time directly in the app by selecting "Delete my data" in App Settings.

4. Third-Party Data and Attributions

To help players discover names during slip submission, the app provides celebrity recommendations sourced from public databases:

  • Wikipedia & Wikidata: Popular figures and descriptions are provided via Wikimedia APIs under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike License.
  • IMDb: Information courtesy of IMDb (https://www.imdb.com). Used with permission.

5. Security

All network communication between your device and the Supabase backend is encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS). Row-level security policies ensure only players in an active room can read game status, and slips remain hidden from guessers until drawn.

6. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue or reach out via our GitHub repository at xozai/celebrity-new.

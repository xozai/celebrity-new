Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Celebrity Celebrity is an iOS party game developed by xozai. We believe multiplayer party games should be fun, simple, and transparent about how data is handled. This privacy policy describes how your information is processed when playing Celebrity Celebrity.

1. Anonymous Accounts and Player Identity

Celebrity Celebrity does not require an email address, phone number, password, or third-party social login.

  • Anonymous Supabase Account: On your first launch, the app creates an anonymous Supabase user account. The authentication session is stored securely in your device's Keychain by the Supabase SDK so you can reconnect across app launches.
  • Player Display Names: When you join or create a room, you enter a display name. While you may choose to enter your real name or a nickname, display names are used solely to identify you on the lobby scoreboard, team assignments, and turn history for players in that room.

2. Game Data and Access Controls

Multiplayer rooms are backed by Supabase with Postgres Row-Level Security (RLS) policies:

  • Room Members Access: Room members (including players and spectators assigned to the room) can read room state, player display names, team scores, and completed turn history. No client can directly insert, update, or delete database rows.
  • Slips and Hidden Pile: Slips entered by players during the submission phase are stored in the database for the active game session. In accordance with game rules, client direct access to the slips table is denied by row-level security. The current slip is returned strictly and atomically only to the active clue-giver via secure SQL functions; guessers, opponents, and spectators cannot view unrevealed slips.

3. Live Wikipedia Search Autocomplete

While typing slip submissions, if the curated recommendation list returns fewer than 6 suggestions, the app sends typed prefixes directly to the Wikimedia API (Wikipedia search) to retrieve long-tail suggestions. These queries transmit only the characters typed, include a descriptive User-Agent, are debounced and cached in memory on device, and do not transmit user or device identifiers.

4. Data Retention and Cleanup

Game session data is temporary and automatically cleaned up:

  • Finished Rooms: Finished rooms are automatically deleted by a scheduled database cleanup job once they are older than one hour.
  • Idle Rooms: Any room idle without activity for 24 hours is automatically purged.
  • Delete My Data: Players can delete their active user data and session at any time directly in the app by selecting "Delete my data" in the app settings screen.
